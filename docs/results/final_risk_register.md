## Evaluation Defense Guidelines

1. **Never Defend by Guessing**: If asked about an untested scenario (e.g. *"How does it perform on hyperspectral 200-band data?"*), respond:  
   *"SatQuery AI currently supports multi-spectral optical and microwave SAR. Hyperspectral band deconvolution is outside our current tool registry and would require specialized spectral unmixing heads."*
2. **Never Inflate Numbers**: Never round 35.0% to "around 40%", or 0.1535 to "around 0.20". Cite the exact figures with pride in their scientific provenance.
3. **Turn Limitations into Proof of Rigor**: Frame every limitation as evidence that SatQuery AI is engineered with adult maturity and rigorous boundary controls, rather than hackathon smoke-and-mirrors.
